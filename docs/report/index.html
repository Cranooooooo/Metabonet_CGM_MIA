MetaboNet · DiM-TS Revision

Does a generative model trained on diabetes data
leak who was in its training set?

This page is the experiment record, not a summary of it. It answers two different questions that are constantly confused with each other, and it keeps their numbers apart: what a realistic attacker can achieve, and how the generator itself responds when one person is added to its training set.

Every figure below carries where it came from, whether it is measured or schematic, and whether the experiment behind it finished.

This page is the argument. For the working record — one tab per day, what was run, what came back and what it decided, including the days that went wrong — see the lab notebook. For the reasoning chain, one entry per decision with the observation that forced it, see the lab diary.

Colour semantics, fixed throughout. In every chart of measured data, orange is the outlier arm, blue is the control arm, and green is a calibration reference (positive control, arithmetic ceiling, chance line). Status uses a reserved palette that is never given to a data series, and always carries a word as well as a colour. Schematics are labelled as schematics and drawn from a fixed seed.

Data. MetaboNet public release, no DUA. Generator DiM-TS. Design derivation in docs/DESIGN.md, stage-by-stage record in docs/HISTORY.md, costings and open items in docs/MEMO.md, the failure catalogue in docs/PITFALLS.md.
